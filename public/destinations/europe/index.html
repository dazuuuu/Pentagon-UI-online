Egypt : Land of Pharaohs and Timeless Wonders

Located in northeastern Africa, Egypt is a country steeped in history, culture, and natural beauty. Known as the cradle of civilization, Egypt offers travelers a journey through ancient wonders, vibrant cities, and breathtaking landscapes.

Historical & Cultural Heritage
Egypt is world-famous for its ancient monuments:

  • Pyramids of Giza – the last surviving wonders of the ancient world
  • Sphinx – iconic limestone statue near Cairo
  • Luxor & Karnak Temples – magnificent ruins along the Nile
  • Valley of the Kings : tombs of Pharaohs including Tutankhamun

Egyptian culture blends Pharaonic, Arab, and Islamic influences, reflected in its architecture, music, and cuisine. Traditional foods include koshari, ful medames, and molokhia.
